Wiktionary-grounded entry pipeline: design and measured night, 2026-10-26

Conclusion (revised the next day, see "Effort decides the writing")
Write every entry from its Wiktionary record, with Opus 5.5 at HIGH effort on the plans. The writer gets the
word's lexemes, genders, plurals, forms, IPA by variety and numbered senses with their region, register and topic
labels, and cites on each sense the evidence senses it covers. Facts then come from the evidence and are checked
and filled by code; only the few facts that still disagree go to one cheap patch call; nothing is reviewed twice.
Against the entries in production today (Opus at high effort, prompt v3, no evidence), this recipe won a blind
comparison 17:9 (10 the same) on 36 fresh words, at about $1.55 per 1,000 entries in plan money ($52 at list).
The first night's recommendation of low effort was wrong: low effort lost to the old entries 9:17. Cheaper
writers did not hold quality either: Haiku 5.5 and Gemini 3.8 Flash lost to Opus 5:19 and 6:19, and Haiku wrote
English definitions into 70 non-English senses.

Effort decides the writing, evidence decides the facts
Blind pairs, new pipeline entry vs the stored entry for the same word, Sonnet judge, 36-37 fresh words, plan CLI:
  new at low effort      new better 9,  old better 17, same 10
  new at medium effort   new better 12, old better 15, same 10
  new at high effort     new better 17, old better 9,  same 10
Plan money per 1,000 (list x 0.03): low $1.16, medium about $1.20, high about $1.55. The judge is noisy (the same
old entries drew 61 errors in one run and 73 in another), but the trend runs one way across three arms. What
evidence adds is not better definitions but facts that can be checked and filled: regions, lexemes split by
gender, verified IPA, inflected forms recognised before any money is spent.

Two kinds of disagreement
Facts have one answer that a source can settle: a lexeme's gender, its plural and forms, how a variety says it
(including per meaning, which the lexeme split carries), where a sense is used. These are compared with Wiktionary
in code (evidence_checks.py); what the evidence settles is filled in; a remaining difference gets one patch call,
and the patch lands only if it verifies. A difference that survives is "disputed", kept with its reason, never
argued into agreement.
Judgments have no single answer: how finely to split senses, which rare sense a learner needs, how to word a
definition, the CEFR level. These are never reviewed in a loop. The writer's choice stands; the only check is
coverage (no common Wiktionary lexeme or sense silently dropped), which is counted, not "fixed". The house rules
that would otherwise be argued are written as rules: a different pronunciation, part of speech, gender or
inflection class is a different lexeme (der/die/das Band, live, bow); everything else is a sense.
Provenance (the citations) is a third, minor kind: a wrong citation costs the regions it would carry, a reader
never sees it, so it never holds an entry.

The pipeline (entry_pipeline.py)
evidence  wiktionary.py: the kaikki.org record for the headword, cached under out/kaikki, reduced to lexemes with
          gender, nominative plural, a few principal forms, the stressed form (Ukrainian), IPA per transcribed
          variety with Wiktionary's own notes (newer, traditional -> older, rare -> less_common), and numbered senses
          with region labels mapped to ISO countries or UN areas (Canary Islands -> ES, Caribbean -> 029).
          Facts are free to reuse; definitions are CC BY-SA and are shown as evidence only, never copied.
write     prompt v5 (prompts/entry_writer/v5.md) plus a one-line reminder after the evidence (cite every sense;
          IPA in house symbols). Arm v5r-evidence in grounded_writer.py. A draft is bought once and reused.
repair    entry_repair.py, free: what has one reading (conjugation 1 -> "1", auxiliary sein -> be, keys the schema
          lacks, gloss {word} -> {text}, gender on English nouns, plural class without its hyphen, domain synonyms,
          Wiktionary notation in the IPA: non-syllabic marks, glottal stops, en-GB ɛ -> e, en-US e -> ɛ, r -> ɹ,
          syllabic n̩ -> ən, uk o -> ɔ).
check     entry_checks.py, free: schema, varieties of the language, IPA inside the inventory, mixed alphabets,
          definitions in English for a non-English entry, duplicate lexemes, region words in text.
fill      evidence_checks.fill, free: a sense whose citations all carry places takes them as regions.
settle    patch_facts.py: fact findings only, one Haiku call, RFC 6902 patch inside the finding's lexeme; applied
          only if the free checks gain no error and the evidence disagrees on fewer facts.
rewrite   once, with the problems listed, when an entry still has a structural error, covers none of the
          evidence's common lexemes, or merges lexemes the evidence keeps apart by gender.
verdict   ready / disputed / held, one ledger line per word with every step's cost.

87% have a record, 7% are inflected forms (Wiktionary names the lemma), 6% have nothing: names (petersburg,
osorio, шевченка), English loans in French and German (pink, undercover), compounds and truncations (Verankerung,
traitance). Derived words Wiktionary files as "form of" another (copine, sida, Ukrainian verbal nouns) carry their
own meaning and are lexemes; only inflections are set aside. German IPA is the thinnest fact (70%).

Verdicts with and without a record (entry_pipeline.py)
inflected   Wiktionary knows the headword only as a form of another word: nothing is written, the ledger names
            the lemma (koordinaten -> Koordinate, expresado -> expresar). These headwords should not be entries.
rejected    the writer returned no lexeme and a reason (a name).
unverified  no record: the writer's knowledge alone, nothing could disagree. German nouns are still checked
            against their last part (Verankerung: -ung, so Fem and -en; Leistungsträger: Träger, so Masc).
ready / disputed / held as before.
Regions come only from the labels a gloss opens with ("(chiefly US) To postpone"), not from kaikki's tags, which
also carry qualifiers of a headword's gender: French caca's "poo" was tagged Louisiana and came out "fr-US only".

Handover to the book processor (the owner's proposal, agreed)
One queue and one switch of its own beside book ingestion, on the processor's plan seats with its pause/resume,
meter pacing and spend guard. A job is one headword, keyed by language, headword, prompt version (v5 + reminder),
schema version (3) and effort, so a rewrite is always deliberate. Steps as in entry_pipeline.py: evidence (shared
kaikki cache), write on a Claude seat at --effort high, repair, check, fill, settle (Haiku through the API or a
plan seat; it ran on 3 of 92 words), one rewrite, verdict. Only "ready" entries are published; "disputed",
"unverified" and "held" go to a review list; "inflected" headwords are removed from the headword lists.

  scale is ever needed, try structured outputs or a Sonnet writer before giving up on the API route.
