Wiktionary-grounded entry pipeline: design and measured night, 2026-10-26

Conclusion
Write every entry from its Wiktionary record. The writer gets the word's lexemes, genders, plurals, forms, IPA by
variety and numbered senses with their region, register and topic labels, and cites on each sense the evidence
senses it covers. Facts then come from the evidence and are checked and filled by code; only the few facts that
still disagree go to one cheap patch call; nothing is reviewed twice. With Opus 5.5 at low effort as the writer,
92 hard and ordinary words came out 89 ready, 3 disputed (genuine pronunciation variants), 0 held. On the plan
that is about $1 per 1,000 entries; at API list price $29.60. Cheaper writers did not hold quality: Haiku 5.5 and
Gemini 3.8 Flash lost blind comparisons to Opus 5:19 and 6:19, and Haiku wrote English definitions into 70
non-English senses. Recommendation: Opus 5.5, effort low, on the plans, through this pipeline.

Two kinds of disagreement
Facts have one answer that a source can settle: a lexeme's gender, its plural and forms, how a variety says it
(including per meaning, which the lexeme split carries), where a sense is used. These are compared with Wiktionary
in code (evidence_checks.py); what the evidence settles is filled in; a remaining difference gets one patch call,
and the patch lands only if it verifies. A difference that survives is "disputed", kept with its reason, never
argued into agreement.
Judgments have no single answer: how finely to split senses, which rare sense a learner needs, how to word a
definition, the CEFR level. These are never reviewed in a loop. The writer's choice stands; the only check is
coverage (no common Wiktionary lexeme or sense silently dropped), which is counted, not "fixed". The house rules
that would otherwise be argued are written as rules: a different pronunciation, part of speech, gender or
inflection class is a different lexeme (der/die/das Band, live, bow); everything else is a sense.
Provenance (the citations) is a third, minor kind: a wrong citation costs the regions it would carry, a reader
never sees it, so it never holds an entry.

The pipeline (entry_pipeline.py)
evidence  wiktionary.py: the kaikki.org record for the headword, cached under out/kaikki, reduced to lexemes with
          gender, nominative plural, a few principal forms, the stressed form (Ukrainian), IPA per transcribed
          variety with Wiktionary's own notes (newer, traditional -> older, rare -> less_common), and numbered senses
          with region labels mapped to ISO countries or UN areas (Canary Islands -> ES, Caribbean -> 029).
          Facts are free to reuse; definitions are CC BY-SA and are shown as evidence only, never copied.
write     prompt v5 (prompts/entry_writer/v5.md) plus a one-line reminder after the evidence (cite every sense;
          IPA in house symbols). Arm v5r-evidence in grounded_writer.py. A draft is bought once and reused.
repair    entry_repair.py, free: what has one reading (conjugation 1 -> "1", auxiliary sein -> be, keys the schema
          lacks, gloss {word} -> {text}, gender on English nouns, plural class without its hyphen, domain synonyms,
          Wiktionary notation in the IPA: non-syllabic marks, glottal stops, en-GB ɛ -> e, en-US e -> ɛ, r -> ɹ,
          syllabic n̩ -> ən, uk o -> ɔ).
check     entry_checks.py, free: schema, varieties of the language, IPA inside the inventory, mixed alphabets,
          definitions in English for a non-English entry, duplicate lexemes, region words in text.
fill      evidence_checks.fill, free: a sense whose citations all carry places takes them as regions.
settle    patch_facts.py: fact findings only, one Haiku call, RFC 6902 patch inside the finding's lexeme; applied
          only if the free checks gain no error and the evidence disagrees on fewer facts.
rewrite   once, with the problems listed, when an entry still has a structural error, covers none of the
          evidence's common lexemes, or merges lexemes the evidence keeps apart by gender.
verdict   ready / disputed / held, one ledger line per word with every step's cost.

Measurements (OpenRouter and Anthropic API, real dollars; plan money is list x 0.03 for Claude)
Writer cost at low effort, 92 words:   Opus 5.5 $29.09/1,000 list (~$0.9 plan); Sonnet 5.5 ~$30 (3 words only);
                                       Gemini 3.8 Flash $6.15; Haiku 5.5 $1.55.
Thinking is the cost: Opus at its default effort (medium) spent 5,374 output tokens on Band; low effort 2,433.
Pipeline verdicts, eval set (92 words: 31 calibration, 33 known-hard, 28 ordinary):
  Opus drafts   89 ready, 3 disputed, 0 held; settling ran on 3 words ($0.0016), 1 rewrite ($0.06).
  Haiku drafts  87 ready, 4 disputed, 1 held at $1.75/1,000 all-in (before the definition-language check).
Validation set (70 fresh ordinary words, Haiku, rules frozen): 60 ready, 8 disputed, 2 held, $1.42/1,000.
Blind pairs, 32 words, Sonnet judge listing material learner-facing errors:
  Haiku pipeline vs Opus: Opus better 19, Haiku 5, same 8; errors 84 vs 69.
  Gemini vs Opus:         Opus better 19, Gemini 6, same 7; errors 78 vs 65.
Calibration facts (calibration_cases.json, Sonnet judge, before four facts were corrected):
  Opus 31 met / 4 missed / 2 contradicted; Gemini 30/3/4; Haiku 27/6/3.
Evidence effect (smoke): Band split into ribbon (Neut, Bänder), bonds (Neut, Bande), volume (Masc, Bände), band
(Fem, Bands, /ˈbɛnt/); table's verb got "propose" mainly en-GB and "postpone" mainly en-US; guagua's bus and baby
got their Caribbean/Canarian and Andean regions. These are the pilot's hardest checkpoints, met in one pass.
Comparator noise found and removed: of Opus's 56 IPA disagreements with Wiktionary, 53 were notation; the rest are
real variants (translation /s/ or /z/, syncopated momentarily, American unstressed ɪ/i in research).

What the night changed in the contract

regions shape and the lexeme split by inflection class earlier the same day.

Open items
- Plans: the production writer belongs in the book processor's plan harness (the owner's proposal): a queue and
  switch of its own, keyed by language, headword, prompt version and schema version, so a rewrite is deliberate.
  Run the Claude CLI at --effort low there; measure the meter per 1,000 entries before a large burn.
- A second plan writer: Codex (Sol, low effort) through the same pipeline has not been measured with evidence.
- Haiku as a cheap writer needs more than prompt reminders (it ignored the definition language); if real-dollar
  scale is ever needed, try structured outputs or a Sonnet writer before giving up on the API route.
- Wiktionary at scale: per-word fetches from kaikki.org are fine for thousands; for a full language, download the
  per-language extract once instead.
- Regions finer than a country (Canary Islands) are written as the country; subdivisions would need a new shape.
- calibration_cases.json facts marked "expected" still need a source before they score anyone.
