# Russian learner’s dictionary review

Of the 18 candidates, one is ready as supplied, twelve can be used after correction, and five should be left out of the example set. The definitions below follow the Russian-specific minimum of four words. Examples use six to fourteen words. CEFR levels and the ordering of senses are editorial estimates.

## Candidate verdicts

| Candidate | Verdict | Editorial finding |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **стоимость** | **use with corrections** | The everyday meaning includes the assessed value of something that is never sold. The labour-theory sense belongs to specialist political economy. Align unstressed **и** with the pronunciation rule and leave countability open. [Ожегов](https://lexicography.online/explanatory/ozhegov/%D1%81/%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%B8%D0%BC%D0%BE%D1%81%D1%82%D1%8C) |
| 2. **мальчик** | **use with corrections** | Add the familiar use for an inexperienced man, mark the servant’s occupation as historical, and drop *девочка* from the strict antonym field. The first definition has five words, which Russian allows. [Кузнецов](https://terminy.info/russian-language/explanatory-dictionary-kuznecova/malchik) |
| 3. **чемпионат** | **use with corrections** | Keep one title competition sense. Generalize *чемпионат мира* into a frame, align **и** with the house IPA, and omit the uncertain Russian derivation from *чемпион*. Черных identifies French *championnat* as the probable source. [Черных](https://www.slovorod.ru/etym-chernykh/_pdf/russ-hist-etym-chernykh-2.pdf) |
| 4. **папа** | **use with corrections** | Adults also say *папа*. Remove *мама* as an antonym and the residence detail from the pope sense. Record the family vocative *пап* and give the old indeclinable *папа́* its own lexeme. [Русский орфографический словарь](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&query=%D0%9F%D0%90%D0%9F%D0%90) · [Vocative form](https://ru.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%BF%D0%B0%D0%BF) |
| 5. **установить** | **use with corrections** | *Установить приложение, срок, контакт* and *причину* require different explanations. Preserve the verb’s stress pattern and aspect partner, and replace the list of objects with usable frames. [Ожегов и Шведова](https://ozhegov.info/slovar/?ex=Y&q=%D0%A3%D0%A1%D0%A2%D0%90%D0%9D%D0%9E%D0%92%D0%98%D0%A2%D0%AC) |
| 6. **искать** | **use with corrections** | The sense “try to obtain” takes both accusative and genitive objects. Include the independent creative use, preserve *ищу́ / и́щешь* and align initial **и** with the house IPA. Finding and searching have different outcomes, so a blanket pairing with *найти* would mislead. [Ожегов и Шведова](https://ozhegov.info/slovar/?q=%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C) · [Падучева on aspect pairs](https://rusohod.narod.ru/index/0-51) |
| 7. **платить** | **use with corrections** | *Платить добром за добро* has an instrumental complement and no direct object. Distinguish reciprocating a deed from suffering a loss, as in *платить здоровьем*, and keep monetary obligations within the literal sense. [Малый академический словарь](https://gufo.me/dict/mas/%D0%BF%D0%BB%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B8%D1%82%D1%8C) |
| 8. **обеспечить** | **use with corrections** | All six inflected forms have the correct stress, including *обеспе́чь*. Add the livelihood reading of *обеспечить семью* alongside supplying something and securing a result. The supply frame also takes an inanimate recipient. [Грамота, forms and senses](https://gramota.ru/meta/obespechit) |
| 9. **морской** | **do not use** | Both definitions use the forbidden family word *море*. The second misses uses such as *морская служба*, and a naval officer illustrates only one branch. Animal names such as *морская свинка* belong with their complete names. [Кузнецов](https://xn--80aacc4bir7b.xn--p1ai/%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8/%D0%B1%D1%82%D1%81/%D0%BC%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B9) |
| 10. **огромный** | **use as it is** | One definition covers size, quantity and intensity, the forest example illustrates size, and *крошечный* gives a genuine scalar opposite. The short forms retain stem stress throughout. [Грамота, forms and senses](https://gramota.ru/meta/ogromnyy) |
| 11. **возле** | **use with corrections** | Keep both parts of speech and the genitive frame. Each gloss repeats synonyms, and the bare adverb is easier to understand when the preceding clause supplies a location. Major dictionaries license that independent use. [Малый академический словарь](https://lexicography.online/explanatory/mas/%D0%B2/%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%BB%D0%B5) |
| 12. **соответственно** | **do not use** | The two ordinary adverbial meanings are useful, but the parenthetical inference needs its own punctuation treatment. The initial vowels also expose a gap in the IPA rule. [Грамота on punctuation](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/263093) · [Prepositional frames](https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/spravochnik-po-punktuatsii/sootvetstvenno-chemu-schem) |
| 13. **вода** | **do not use** | Keep *во́ду* and the informal “empty words” sense. The list misses *сесть на воду* (the surface) and *отошли воды* (plural, childbirth). The territorial gloss also misses *подземные воды*. Scope mass and plural information by meaning. [Активный словарь русского языка](https://ruslang.ru/sites/default/files/2024-02/%D0%90%D0%BA%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%BD%D1%8B%D0%B9%20%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%80%D1%8C%20%D1%80%D1%83%D1%81%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%20%D1%8F%D0%B7%D1%8B%D0%BA%D0%B0.%20%D0%A2%D0%BE%D0%BC%202.pdf) · [Dictionary comparison for воды](https://www.vsu.ru/ru/university/structure/communicate/pdf/comp-stud/comp-stud17.pdf) |
| 14. **солнце** | **do not use** | The IPA correctly omits **л**, but the Earth-specific definition cannot explain *два солнца*. Distinguish the astronomical proper name *Солнце*, ordinary *солнце*, and the light it gives. The affectionate example is sound, but the light sense unnecessarily requires brightness and warmth. [Academic spelling rules](https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/pravila-russkoy-orfografii-i-punktuatsii/astronomicheskie-nazvaniya) · [Ожегов и Шведова](https://ozhegov.info/slovar/?ex=Y&q=%D0%A1%D0%9E%D0%9B%D0%9D%D0%A6%D0%95) |
| 15. **удар** | **do not use** | *Прикосновение* understates an impact. Account for *удары сердца* and sounds such as thunder, remove the extra IPA key *notation*, and qualify the older illness sense. Equating every *инсульт* with a brain haemorrhage is medically inaccurate. [Кузнецов](https://xn--80aacc4bir7b.xn--p1ai/%D1%81%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%B8/%D0%B1%D1%82%D1%81/%D1%83%D0%B4%D0%B0%D1%80) · [WHO on stroke](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/stroke) |
| 16. **подарок** | **use with corrections** | A lesson or a trip can be a gift. Broaden the literal gloss, remove the repeated *неожиданное / неожиданная* from the figurative one, and replace the fixed collocation *подарок судьбы* with a productive frame. [Ожегов и Шведова](https://www.wordinf.ru/t/podarok) |
| 17. **дважды** | **use with corrections** | The arithmetic use is independently numbered. *Дважды два — четыре* illustrates multiplication, whereas the telephone example illustrates two occasions. A reusable frame can take any numeral. [Большой универсальный словарь](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&page=2&query=%D0%94%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%B6%D0%B4%D1%8B&simple=0) |
| 18. **насколько** | **use with corrections** | *Насколько я знаю* limits a claim through the ordinary meaning of degree. Merge the two senses, remove the doubled gloss, and put the correlative construction beside the degree use. [Малый академический словарь](https://gufo.me/dict/mas/%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BA%D0%BE) |

## Four decisions about the Russian format

### 1. Pronunciation

Keep broad Moscow IPA alongside the stressed Cyrillic reading. *Солнце* /ˈson.tsə/ teaches a silent letter, *хлеб* /ˈxlʲep/ teaches final devoicing, and *вода* /vɐ.ˈda/ teaches reduction. The candidates mix conventions for unstressed **и**. Under the written rule, use /i/ in *стоимость*, *мальчик*, the **пи** of *чемпионат*, and the start of *искать*. The **че** of *чемпионат* still has /ɪ/. A more phonetic transcription can legitimately give [ɪ] for unstressed **и**. [Phonetic example for искать](https://ru.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C)

Add a rule for adjacent unstressed vowels before using *соответственно* as a test. The two pretonic vowels in **соот-** can both be [ɐ], and consonant length in **-нн-** can vary. A fully explicit geminate should use the specified /ː/ convention consistently. [Валгина et al. on unstressed vowel sequences](https://pedlib.ru/Books/6/0262/6_0262-124.shtml) · [Pronunciation of соответственно](https://ru.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D1%81%D0%BE%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B5%D1%82%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%BE)

### 2. Ё and е

Display *ёлка* and *ребёнок* with **ё** and index *елка* and *ребенок* as undotted spellings of those lexemes. Both spellings have the same pronunciation and need no regional tags. An exact **е** spelling can also have an independent analysis, as in *все* and *всё*. Preserve both lookup results. None of the 18 headword lemmas contains **ё**, so the sample does not actually test the spelling field. [Academic spelling rules, § 5](https://gramota.ru/biblioteka/spravochniki/pravila-russkoy-orfografii-i-punktuatsii/upotreblenie-bukvy-e-v-tekstakh-raznogo-naznacheniya)

### 3. Principal forms

Keep the three noun forms and six verb forms for words that have them. They expose endings, stem changes and stress economically. *Воды́ / во́ды / во́ду* shows the value of exceptional accusatives, and *плачу́ / пла́тишь / плати́* shows why one present-tense cell cannot stand in for the others. Also record unpredictable short forms, oblique stress, suppletion and common special forms of address, such as *пап*. A past neuter or plural is worth adding when its stress or stem cannot be predicted. Mark a missing cell for an impersonal or defective verb through a grammar note.

Use the simple future for perfectives and the present for ordinary imperfectives. The analytic future *буду платить* is predictable. Identical spellings can represent several cells. In *папы*, genitive singular and nominative plural deserve separate records because both case and number change. An indeclinable noun can use a single *invariable* declaration. [Perfective paradigm for обеспечить](https://gramota.ru/meta/obespechit)

### 4. Count form, second locative and partitive

| Field | Learner-facing treatment | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `Number=Count` | Reserve it for a distinctive numeral-governed form, including a stress-only difference. State which numerals select it. After *два, три, четыре* in direct-case counting phrases, the ordinary form is taught as genitive singular. Compound numerals ending in 2–4 follow this pattern except those ending in 12–14. | *два часа́* beside *о́коло ча́са* and *пять часо́в*. *Два стола́* uses the ordinary form found in *нет стола́*. |
| `Case=Loc` | Show the preposition and the spatial reading in a note. Russian UD usage also gives the ordinary prepositional case this tag, so the tag alone cannot identify the second locative. | *в лесу́* for location, *о ле́се* for the topic of conversation. |
| `Case=Par` | Show the second-genitive form where it survives and give the ordinary genitive alongside it. | *выпить ча́ю* and *выпить ча́я* both express an indefinite quantity. |

These distinctions are useful precisely where the learner must choose a different form. Give *пять челове́к* under *человек* and *пять лет* under *год*, with the numeral context. Those are different problems from the stress shift in *два часа́*. Count is a number value. The second locative and partitive are restricted case forms. In JSON, keep the single noun form in *form* and its conditioning preposition or numeral in *note*. [UD Number, including Russian count forms](https://universaldependencies.org/u/feat/Number.html) · [UD Case](https://universaldependencies.org/u/feat/Case.html) · [Грамота on в лесу and о лесе](https://gramota.ru/spravka/vopros/203694)
