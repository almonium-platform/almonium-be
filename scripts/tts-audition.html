What these tests establish

Google demos help judge voice character. They are published vendor recordings, not evidence that our app’s IPA controls work. Our custom tests use the exact visible text and show any requested IPA. “Accepted by API” does not mean pronunciation has been checked by a speaker.

For apple, compare raw text and IPA for the same intended pronunciation. For read, raw text is ambiguous; the two overrides deliberately request present /riːd/ and past /rɛd/. They should differ. German and Ukrainian samples here use raw text; Ukrainian IPA overrides are not claimed to be supported.

Duration, decoding, level and near-full-scale checks are technical diagnostics. They cannot certify correct phonemes, stress or naturalness. Different hashes are normal and do not mean the pronunciation changed.

Recordings remain local. See GUIDE.md and manifest.json in this folder for sources, request settings, costs and technical measurements.
